# Methods

`PdSample` interprets one genome. Every call is made from that sample's BAM (`EnsureBam` converts a CRAM input first). Scores are additive points from `config/config.yaml`. They are a triage rank in the style of ACMG evidence categories, and they are not an ACMG/AMP classification. Scored LoF structural variants get `null_variant` points via `transcript_ablation` plus a configurable `sv_dosage_lof` bonus (default 8), because SVs do not carry ClinVar/CADD/REVEL/AVI in-silico fields used for SNVs.

## Callers

Small variants are single-sample HaplotypeCaller GVCFs, genotyped with GenotypeGVCFs and filtered with the GATK single-sample hard-filter expressions documented in the README. VQSR is a cohort fit, and no CNN model is bundled, so hard filters are the filter used here. The default interval list is the panel gene-span BED.

GATK-SV single-sample (v1.1.1, Scramble on / MELT off) runs by default. Optional workspace `sv_joint_vcf` supplies a cohort joint-called SV VCF: when `sample_id` is in the header the workflow subsets that sample and skips single-sample GATK-SV; when absent it falls back to `GATKSVPipelineSingleSample`. The reference panel and hg38 resource files are required inputs for the single-sample path, taken from the public 1KG bundle that tag defines (`gatk-sv-ref-panel-1kg-v1-0-1`) or from a matched panel in workspace data. Overlap uses the gene and exon BEDs under `resources/panel/`. SV INFO/AF (or CN_NONREF_FREQ) is stored as `cohort_af` for annotation only and is not used as a hard frequency filter — a rare gnomAD SV that is enriched in a small PD callset must still pass. `sv_panel_af` is reserved for a true GATK-SV reference-panel AF when available and is then filtered with the same ceiling as gnomAD AF. `pdvar assess` requires the SV VCF; it does not treat a missing file as a skipped call. A sample that is absent from an existing SV VCF header is still `SV not assessed` on that row of the coverage table.

ExpansionHunter uses the vendored 19-locus catalog. A repeat count is labelled `str_expanded` only when `str_loci.<locus>.pathogenic_min_repeats` and `citation` are both set and `max(REPCN) ≥` that floor. Cutoffs are GeneReviews-sourced triage floors for PD / adult ataxia differentials (including FXTAS premutation at FMR1 ≥55). Motif purity, CAT/AGG interruptions, combined CTA+CTG sizing for SCA8, and biallelic requirement (RFC1, CSTB, FXN) are not modelled. FGF14, BEAN1, LRP12, and NOTCH2NLC have config entries but are not in the vendored EH catalog yet; BEAN1 and LRP12 keep null repeat floors until units are defined.

Gauchian copy number 0, or an explicit deletion, becomes a homozygous `transcript_ablation` in `GBA1`. A recombinant allele stays a structural variant unless the Gauchian file itself marks it pathogenic.

MitochondriaPipeline 4.7.0.0 calls chrM. Carried mtDNA variants are labelled `mtdna_variant`. That status is visible on the candidate list and does not mark the sample solved.

## Annotation and ranking

VEP 116 `--pick` supplies consequence, symbol, and HGVS. Academic **dbNSFP 5.4a** (VEP-ready `dbNSFP5.4a_grch38.gz` + `.tbi` via `dbnsfp` / `dbnsfp_index`) supplies missense/splice `CADD_phred`, `REVEL_score`, Eigen, MutationTaster, fathmm-XF, `clinvar_clnsig`, and `gnomAD4.1_joint_AF` / `gnomAD4.1_joint_POPMAX_AF` (classic `FATHMM_*` is not in 5.4a). There are no separate gnomAD or ClinVar VEP `--custom` inputs. `LiftGnomadInfo` copies those AF fields from CSQ into `INFO/gnomAD_AF` / `AF_popmax` for Introme. Optional genome-wide CADD/REVEL plugin inputs remain as a fallback. SIFT and PolyPhen come from dbNSFP (`SIFT_*` / `Polyphen2_HDIV_*`); cache-native VEP `--sift/--polyphen` is off by default (`vep_sift_polyphen`). Splice triage uses Introme (not a VEP SpliceAI column). `pdvar` maps `clinvar_clnsig` to `clinvar_class`. Missing gnomAD AF is treated as rare.

AlphaGenome Atlas AVI scores come from `workflow/scripts/annotate_avi.py`, a local Tabix lookup (`pysam==0.24.1`) of PASS SNVs in the normalized VCF against the precomputed AlphaGenome AVI SNV table (https://alphagenome.google/downloads). A row is used only when CHROM, POS, REF, and ALT all match, and the task fails if REF disagrees at more than 10% of looked-up positions. The table is GRCh38 and SNV-only. `avi_raw` and `avi_phred` are taken from the table. The table has no CDF column, so `avi_quantile` is `1 - 10^(-Phred/10)` from the published Phred. Phred is `-10 * log10(1 - CDF)` against all genome-wide SNVs: 20 is the top 1% and 30 is the top 0.1% (Cheng et al., medRxiv, 2026, doi:10.64898/2026.09.16.26363192; the model is Avsec et al., Nature 649:1206, 2026, doi:10.1038/s41586-025-10014-0). Those two bands add 1 and 3 triage points, the same scale as the moderate and high CADD bands, and a higher Phred breaks ties inside a tier. They are rank bands, not a pathogenicity cutoff. Indels and an unset `avi_table` leave `avi_phred` empty and add no points. Atlas outputs are for research modelling. They are not a diagnostic result and are not for training other models.

Introme v2 (`d6da48bc`) scores splice consequences on the panel BED (including SpliceAI). When dbNSFP annotated gnomAD AF, `LiftGnomadInfo` exposes `INFO/gnomAD_AF` for Introme's rare filter. A second Introme allele is added inside the same sample for an AR gene that already has a qualifying hit, and only when `gnomad_af` is missing or at or below `lookup_max_gnomad_af` (0.01).

Inheritance uses the panel CSV (`GBA` aliased to `GBA1`). Two qualifying heterozygous alleles in an AR gene are `AR_comphet_candidate` unless both genotypes are phased (`|`) and share a `PS` tag, in which case the pair is `AR_comphet_phased` or collapsed to a single het. A LoF / exon-disrupting SV (DEL or intragenic DUP) plus a coding or ClinVar P/LP allele in the same AR gene are paired as `AR_comphet_candidate` even when the small variant was initially a single het; same-gene Introme-only intron "solves" are demoted to `AR_intronic_candidate` and ranked below coding/SV hits. Statistical phasing is disabled. A biallelic finding in a risk gene such as GBA1 is `risk_biallelic` and counts as partially explained. TTN null alleles are tagged `incidental` (`TTN_truncation`). ClinVar is left as reported, and the row is excluded from solved and partial liability.

Small-variant depth QC uses `min_depth` (default 10). Depth-only failures can still be kept when GQ and allele balance pass: HIGH-impact panel alleles within `depth_rescue_near_miss_factor` of the floor (default 2×, so depth ≥ 5), or any keep-consequence allele inside a same-sample LoF / exon-disrupting deletion down to `depth_rescue_lof_del_min_depth` (default 5). Rescued rows carry `depth_rescued` and `depth_rescue_reason` (`near_miss`, `lof_del`, or both).

## Phenotype concordance

`config/gene_phenotypes.yaml` includes profiles for `str_loci` expansion genes (SCA, FXTAS, C9orf72, RFC1, NIID, SCA27B, and related phenocopies) so cited STR expansions can receive `clinical_match` when onset or symptom keywords fit.

Optional sample columns `age_onset`, `symptoms`, and `ethnicity`, and an optional `phenotype_report` text file, are copied into the Assess phenotype block when the patient has them. The report layout is the GP2-style sheet: `GP2ID`, sex, age at sample collection, PD status, levodopa, age at levodopa start, age at diagnosis, diagnosis type, age at onset, then exposures and comorbidities grouped as high or lower relevance. `NA` fields are dropped. The same text pasted into `symptoms` is parsed as that sheet. Explicit age, symptoms, and ancestry override the report. `pdvar` compares age at onset, symptom keywords, and ancestry with `config/gene_phenotypes.yaml`. Levodopa recorded as yes is a biomarker note on early-onset recessive genes. Comorbidity lines are printed on the report and are not gene-profile keywords. Onset inside the profile range, a symptom keyword that hits a key or atypical feature, and an enriched ancestry each count toward `phenotype_score`. `clinical_match` is `matched` when any of those agree, `unmatched` when onset is outside the range and nothing else agrees, `uninformative` when the gene has a profile but the fields neither confirm nor refute it, `no_profile` when the gene has no profile, and `not_assessed` when the three columns are blank. The report lists risk candidates (`risk_carrier`, `risk_biallelic`, `moi` RISK, and `risk_allele_exception`) with that match. Concordance does not add evidence points. A higher `phenotype_score` breaks ties after tier and AlphaGenome Phred. Profiles cover every gene in the panel CSV, grouped as typical Mendelian PD, atypical parkinsonism, risk genes, low-evidence candidates, and differential diagnoses (dystonia, NBIA, PFBC, dementia/ALS, ataxia/HSP, mitochondrial, lysosomal/leukodystrophy, neurodevelopmental). A profile without `onset_range` is not scored on age. The profiles are a starter literature synthesis from GeneReviews, OMIM, and MDSGene (`config/gene_phenotypes.SOURCES.md`), not a systematic review, and need expert clinical review before use beyond triage.

Isoform and expression weights stay neutral while `expression.min_brain_pext`, `min_gtex_tpm`, and `unexpressed_weight` are null. Biomarker multipliers stay 1 while their citations are null. `explained_liability` is numeric only when `liability.penetrance` has a value and a citation. The shipped penetrance map is empty.
